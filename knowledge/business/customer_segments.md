# Customer Segments

## Enterprise
Enterprise customers are organisations with more than 500 employees. They buy in large
quantities, often under annual procurement cycles, so their orders are lumpy and sensitive to
budget freezes.

## SMB
SMB (small and medium business) customers have between 10 and 500 employees.

## Consumer
Consumer customers are individuals buying for personal use, typically one to three units per order.
