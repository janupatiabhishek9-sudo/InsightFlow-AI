# Company KPIs

## Primary KPI
Revenue is the primary KPI. When a question does not name a metric, analyses should use revenue.
Profit is the secondary KPI and is used for margin discussions.

## KPI list
- Revenue: net revenue after discounts (see revenue.md).
- Profit: revenue minus cost of goods sold.
- Orders: number of order lines in the period.
- Units: total quantity sold.
- Average order value (AOV): revenue divided by orders.
- Effective discount: 1 - revenue / gross revenue, where gross revenue = quantity x unit price.

## Period comparisons
The default comparison for "why did X change" questions is the immediately preceding period of
equal length (for a quarter, the previous quarter). Year-over-year comparisons use the same
period of the prior year and must be requested explicitly ("vs last year", "YoY").
