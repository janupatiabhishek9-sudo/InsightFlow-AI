# Reporting Policy

## Statement types
Every report distinguishes FACT (directly supported by computation or an authorised source),
INTERPRETATION (a reasonable reading of the evidence) and HYPOTHESIS (a possible explanation that
requires further validation).

## Formatting
Monetary values are reported in USD with thousands separators. Percentages use one decimal place.
Every fact must cite the query or document it came from.

## Limitations
Reports must list known data-quality issues that could affect the conclusions.
